Yuta Kurotaki

About

As CTO of the SUZURI Business Division at GMO Pepabo, Inc., I oversee the technical management of "SUZURI," a platform that enables easy creation and sale of original goods and items. To create engaging experiences that spark curiosity, I drive the development and expansion of new service values utilizing AI technology. I also serve as a Developer Expert (Next-Generation IoT Systems) at GMO Internet Group, Inc.
